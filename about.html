About Us

Our Objectives & Advocacy

Raising the status of LGBTQ community in Kenya through awareness & educational resources
Improving community self-esteem, body image, and holistic mental health
Providing a safe space, emergency shelter, and legal navigation for persecuted members
Equipping members with confidence and advocacy tools to demand dignity & justice

We take an intersectional approach to our work and believe that fighting one form of oppression means fighting all forms of oppression. We are committed to promoting equality and respect for all people, irrespective of their sexual orientation or gender identities.

Enhance knowledge and skills to increase womxn’s agency, to enhance womxn’s ability to navigate their lives safely and maximize living their lives without fear of discrimination or violence.

We work toward creating and maintaining a safe, inclusive, and equitable environment for the LGBTQIA+ community. Our Safe Shelter aims to sustain visibility and a sense of community throughout our region.

R NetworQ exists to break that isolation. We are a lifeline for LGBTQ+ migrants escaping Uganda's brutal crackdown—providing emergency shelter, legal navigation, psychosocial care, and the critical infrastructure of safety that no one else is building.

Our Core Mission

Breaking Isolation & Building Safety

R NetworQ exists to break that isolation. We are a lifeline for LGBTQ+ migrants escaping Uganda's brutal crackdown—providing emergency shelter, legal navigation, psychosocial care, and safety infrastructure.

Our beneficiaries are not victims waiting to be saved. They are survivors of systematic state violence who deserve dignity, documentation, and a fighting chance at life. We meet them where they are—hiding in safe houses, crossing borders in darkness, waiting in legal limbo—and we stay.

Cross-Border Vulnerability

Along the Kenya-Uganda crossing, queer sex workers exist in a zone of extreme vulnerability—criminalized by the state, exploited by clients, harassed by police, and abandoned by aid systems that refuse to see them.

For Trans+ individuals and queer people in transit, sex work is often the only survival strategy available in a landscape that denies them employment, housing, healthcare, and legal protection. Every transaction carries risk: violence, infection, arrest, deportation. Every night is a calculation between hunger and harm.
