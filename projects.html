Our Key Projects

Ensuring LGBTQ Visibility, Protection & Affirmation

Repro Justice

In many African countries, including Kenya, sexual behaviour between consenting adults of the same sex/gender is illegal which results in limited availability and access to health services and information for sexual and gender minorities.

The Kenyan Constitution guarantees health rights but does not explicitly mention sexual and gender minorities. Members of the community face higher burdens of STDs/HIV and mental health challenges without adequate affirming care.

Safe Shelter & Crisis Infrastructure

The Space provides a platform for high-risk members to be with other youth who identify in similar ways, receive affirming health education, civic engagement opportunities, and experience belonging.

We provide emergency shelter, legal navigation, psychosocial care, and safety infrastructure for survivors escaping systematic state violence.

Digital Rights & Online Protection

Educate vulnerable LGBTIQ members to prevent online and tech-facilitated violence.
Advocate against homophobia and unjust legal frameworks via digital campaigns.
Equip members with cybersecurity skills to navigate the internet safely.

Art Therapy & Storytelling

Art and cultural work are powerful mediums for resistance, cultural change, and healing. Story-telling facilitates self-expression, trauma recovery, and creative exploration through:

Painting Creative Writing Mobile Documentaries Conceptual Photography

Cross-Border & Transit Crisis Support

Along the Kenya-Uganda crossing, queer sex workers exist in a zone of extreme vulnerability—criminalized by the state, exploited by clients, harassed by police, and abandoned by aid systems.

R NetworQ meets them where they are—hiding in safe houses, crossing borders in darkness, waiting in legal limbo—and we stay.
